      Help

      The short version of everything TowPacket does.

      How do I start a case?

      On the New Case tab, enter the vehicle off the tow ticket — VIN and tow date are what start the clock. In the live product you snap a photo of the ticket and the fields fill from it. The answer to "How did it get here?" picks the legal track: law-enforcement directed and § 954A tows run under 47 O.S. § 954A / 42 O.S. § 91A; everything else runs under the possessory-lien process at 42 O.S. § 91.

      What do the statutes on every date mean?

      Every deadline TowPacket computes names the Oklahoma statute it comes from — for example, the Notice of Possessory Lien must be mailed no later than 60 days after the first services 42 O.S. § 91 A.4, and storage charges may only be assessed beginning the day that notice is mailed 42 O.S. § 91 A.5. Where the law or the current state form can't be sourced, the app shows a verify flag instead of a confident date — check the current Service Oklahoma form before acting on a flagged step.

      What does TowPacket do — and not do?

      TowPacket prepares: timelines, notice letters, mail and proof logs, and the sale & title packet. It does not file anything, sign anything, certify anything, or hold your Service Oklahoma credentials — the owner signs (the lien notice signature is notarized), files, and pays the state. It is a preparation tool, not legal advice; for a disputed case, talk to an Oklahoma attorney.

      How do the notices work?

      On the Notices tab, pick the case and the letter. The letter fills from the case file with the statute's content checklist beside it. Print it, sign and notarize where the checklist says, mail it both ways the statute requires (first-class and certified, return receipt requested), then log the mailings — the certified date drives the timeline.

      What is the "protected storage" number?

      Under 42 O.S. § 91 A.5, storage charges on the § 91 track may only be assessed beginning the day the Notice of Possessory Lien is mailed. Days before that mailing are unprotected — the car sat, but the storage isn't chargeable under the lien. Mail the notice early and the protected count starts early. Storage is also capped: the maximum allowable compensation for storage shall not exceed the fees established by the Corporation Commission for nonconsensual tows 42 O.S. § 91 A.5 — TowPacket uses your entered rate and flags the cap; verify your rate against the current Commission schedule.

      Where does my data live? How do I back it up?

      In this demo edition, everything lives on this device, in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded. Export the lot board CSV from the Lot Board to keep a dated copy, print packets as you complete them, and use Reset below to restore the fictional sample data or Clear to empty the app. Clearing your browser's site data erases the cases, so export before you do.
